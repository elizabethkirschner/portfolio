About Me

Hello! I'm an aspiring UX researcher with a background in computer science and game development. I am a second year graduate student at Claremont Graduate University pursuing an M.A. in Applied Cognitive Psychology: User Experience. I completed my undergraduate studies at Worcester Polytechnic Institute where I earned a B.S. in Computer Science and a B.S. Interactive Media and Game Development. My career interests are in user experience research focusing on accessibility and inclusion.

I am currently seeking full time employment upon the completion of my degree in May, 2023. Please reach out!
